- **CLI 契约**：`semantic_service_core.py <ontology目录> --ask "问题" [--json]`；输出结构化裁决 `{status: answered|refused_out_of_surface|refused_missing|refused_banned, waterfall_level, anchor, hits:[{id,model,name}], citations, scaffold_commands}`；--json 机读，人类面简报。退出码 0 answered / 1 refused（拒答也是有效裁决）/ 2 配置错误。
